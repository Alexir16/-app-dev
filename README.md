# My Favorite Series: WandaVision

## About the Series

**WandaVision** is a *superhero and psychological thriller* television series produced by Marvel Studios. It follows Wanda Maximoff and Vision as they live a seemingly perfect suburban life while strange events begin to happen around them.

---

## Why I Like It

I like WandaVision because it has a unique storyline, interesting mysteries, and unexpected plot twists. I also enjoy how each episode shows a different television era while slowly revealing what is really happening.

## My Favorite Characters

- **Wanda Maximoff** - A powerful superhero who can manipulate reality.
- **Vision** - An android with human emotions and intelligence.
- **Agatha Harkness** - A mysterious witch with powerful magic.
- **Monica Rambeau** - An agent who becomes involved in the events surrounding Westview.
